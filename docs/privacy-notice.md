**Last updated:** [date of publication]

Ramboo CELE App is a free practice tool for the Civil Engineering Licensure
Examination, made by Darkwiz Lab in the Philippines. This notice explains what
happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **your
practice data stays on your device, and we do not collect personal
information.**

## What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following in your browser's own storage, on your device:

- your answers, scores and timing;
- your mistake bank and the list of problems you have already seen;
- your settings, such as theme.

This information is **not sent to us or to anyone else**. We cannot see it.
There are no accounts, so we do not ask for your name, email address or any
other identifying detail.

Because the data lives in your browser, it is removed if you clear your
browser's site data, and it does not follow you to another device. You can
download a backup file from the app and load it on another device yourself.

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no advertising trackers.
- No cookies that identify you.
- No selling or sharing of user data — we have none to share.

## Hosting

The app is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical
information when your browser requests a page, including your IP address, and
handles it under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

## Donations

Donations are optional. If you donate through GCash, Maya or Ko-fi, the payment
is handled by that provider under its own privacy policy. We see only what the
provider shows to a recipient, such as the amount and the name you chose to
display, and we use it only to acknowledge and account for the donation.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right
to be informed about, to access, to correct, to object to, and to request
erasure of personal information held about you, to receive a copy of it, and
to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Since the app holds your practice data only on your own device, you exercise
most of these directly: view it in the app, export it with the backup feature,
or erase it with "Reset" or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

## Changes to this notice

When accounts, cloud sync or payments are introduced, this notice will be
updated **before** those features go live, and the app will tell you about the
change. Older versions will remain available in the project's public
repository.
