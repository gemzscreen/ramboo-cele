# Original Content Policy

The full policy is `ROADMAP.md` §3 and is non-negotiable. This page is the
working checklist; if the two ever disagree, the roadmap wins.

## Before a template goes live

1. Written fresh from the archetype — new scenario, own wording.
2. Figure generated from the template variables — never traced or redrawn from
   a source.
3. Solved independently and coded as a pure solver function.
4. Passed the harness (stages 1–3, and stage 4 locally) including a
   hand-checked reference instance.
5. Tagged with its concept and code reference (NSCP 2015 section or standard
   formula).

## From past boards and review booklets

- **May use:** the topic, concept and how often it appears; the generic
  archetype; exam structure facts (situations of 3, item counts, timing).
- **Never use:** stem wording or sentence structure, answer choices, figures,
  solution text or sequence, branding. Changing only the numbers is still a copy.

## This repository is public

Never commit past-board PDFs, the legacy 4.9.1 bank, or any third-party
material. They live in the sibling folder `../ramboo-private/`.

## Labels

Say "board-style" or "patterned after recurring board topics". Never "actual
board question" or "official PRC key". Every generated set carries the standard
disclaimer:

> The problems in this test questionnaire are not exact copies of the actual
> board exam. They are designed to closely resemble the style, structure, and
> level of difficulty of real board exam questions to help examinees prepare
> effectively.
