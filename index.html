About

Ramboo CELE App is a free practice tool for the Civil Engineering Licensure Examination, covering PSAD, MSTE and HGE. It is in development: the problem bank is small and features are still being added.

How the problems are made

Every problem is written from scratch for this app. Each one is a pattern with its own solver, so the values change every time and the answer is computed by code, then checked by an automated test run before release. The wrong choices come from named common mistakes, so a miss tells you what went wrong.

Topics follow the PRC program for the examination. The problems are board-style. They are not past board questions, and the answers are not an official PRC key.

Disclaimer

The problems in this app are not exact copies of the actual board exam. They are designed to closely resemble the style, structure, and level of difficulty of real board exam questions to help examinees prepare effectively.

Check any formula or code provision against the NSCP and your own references before relying on it. If an answer looks wrong, it may be: this is an early version.

Your data

There are no accounts. Your scores, mistakes and theme choice are kept in this browser only and are not sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's site data removes them. Use Export backup on the Progress tab to keep a copy or move to another device.

Install

On a phone, open the browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen" to use the app like any other. It still needs a connection to load.
